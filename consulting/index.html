Consulting

Start with the situation, not the service.

I help companies find clarity, build the right delivery setup and move important data and digital initiatives forward.

I’m usually brought in when…

Good people. Important initiative. Something is not working.

You may have the ambition, technology and team, but priorities are unclear, delivery is slowing down, ownership is fuzzy or business and technology are not aligned.

01

Unclear direction

The initiative exists, but scope, priorities or roadmap are unclear.

02

Delivery challenges

The team exists, but dependencies, ownership or execution are getting in the way.

03

Need for leadership

You need experienced Product, Project or Delivery leadership without another full-time hire.

04

Business–tech gap

Business and technology are not aligned and the initiative is not producing the expected value.

Ways to work with me

Five ways to move forward.

Each engagement can stand on its own. The right starting point depends on where the initiative is today.

01

Review the Situation

Data & Digital Delivery Review

When you need this: You know delivery is not working as expected, but the root cause is unclear.

How I help: I review priorities, ownership, risks, dependencies and ways of working, then turn the findings into a practical 30/60/90-day action plan.

Team Building & Delivery Setup
