Global Sourcing Analytics Modernization

Leading the end-to-end delivery of a new global sourcing data warehouse and Power BI dashboard built from scratch for an S/4HANA migration.

Role
Project Lead
Delivery
From Day Zero
Scale
900+ Global Users
Stack
Databricks · SAP

1. Executive Summary & Business Context

Atlas Copco needed a new sourcing analytics solution as part of its ERP migration. The product serves sourcing decisions across delivery performance, cost and payables, quality, and rejections while supporting stakeholders from factory users to vice presidents.

2. Architecture & Solution Strategy

I owned delivery end to end, including business analysis, scope, roadmap, backlog, budget and delivery planning. I coordinated a six-person international team and aligned KPI definitions with vice presidents when seven divisions had competing requirements.

// Architecture Pipeline Summary

SAP → S/4HANA → Databricks sourcing data warehouse → Power BI dashboard

3. Measurable Outcomes & Business Impact

  • Delivered a production sourcing data warehouse and Power BI dashboard from scratch.
  • Supported a global footprint of approximately 900 users, seven divisions and 25 companies.
  • Improved reporting speed, data availability and reliability while the previous dashboard remained operational.
