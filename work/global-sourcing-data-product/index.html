Atlas Copco · Data & Transformation

Global Sourcing Analytics Modernization

I led the end-to-end delivery of a new global sourcing data warehouse and Power BI product, built from day zero to support an enterprise S/4HANA migration.

Project Lead7 divisions25 companiesMulti-country team
Challenge

Build the new sourcing analytics foundation without interrupting the existing service.

The ERP migration created a fixed business need: a new analytics product had to be designed for the future S/4HANA landscape while the previous dashboard continued to serve operational users.

Complexity

One product, seven divisions, different operational realities.

  • Shared sourcing definitions across seven divisions and 25 companies.
  • SAP data, S/4HANA migration, data quality and the analytical model evolving together.
  • Distributed specialists across China, India, Bulgaria, Czechia and Belgium.
  • Service continuity while the new product was designed and launched.
My role

Project Lead, from day zero to production.

I was accountable for delivery end to end and also performed the business-analysis leadership needed to shape the product—aligning scope, KPI ownership, stakeholders, dependencies and execution.

Solution

A governed path from ERP source to sourcing decisions.

The delivery combined a sourcing-focused Databricks warehouse with a Power BI presentation layer designed around agreed global KPIs, using milestone-based governance for ERP dependencies and an iterative backlog for engineering, BI and validation.

Outcome

A new production analytics capability built from scratch.

  • Sourcing-focused Databricks data warehouse delivered into production.
  • Power BI dashboard supporting global sourcing performance and control.
  • Improved reporting speed, availability and reliability for the new ERP landscape.
