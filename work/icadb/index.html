ICADB: Iran Comprehensive Agriculture Database

Managing the delivery of a nationwide BI platform built from scratch for specialists and experts across Iran’s Ministry of Agriculture organizations.

Role
BI Project Manager
Entity
Iran Ministry of Agriculture
Sponsor
Deputy Minister
Scope
Nationwide
01 / Context

A Comprehensive Agricultural Information Platform

The Iran Comprehensive Agriculture Database was commissioned as a national platform for Ministry specialists and experts, covering agricultural organizations across the country.

The initiative was sponsored by the Deputy Minister of Agriculture and built from scratch to provide a shared analytical foundation at nationwide scale.

02 / Delivery

End-to-End Business Intelligence Delivery

As BI Project Manager, I was accountable for project delivery across a multidisciplinary team of Data Engineers, BI Engineers, Business Analysts and Data Scientists:

  • Data foundation: Delivered the data warehouse and ETL pipelines.
  • Analytics layer: Delivered semantic models, dashboards and operational reports.
  • Cross-functional execution: Coordinated engineering, analytics, business analysis and data-science work.
  • National rollout: Prepared the platform for hundreds of specialist users and millions of rows of data across Iran.
