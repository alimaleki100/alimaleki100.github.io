The mandate

Create a shared analytical foundation for agriculture at national scale.

The Ministry needed a comprehensive BI platform for specialists and experts working across agricultural organizations throughout Iran.

Sponsored by the Deputy Minister of Agriculture, the initiative required a new data and analytics capability to be assembled and delivered from scratch—not an upgrade of an existing platform.

Leadership differentiator

I built the delivery capability before leading the delivery.

I identified the delivery disciplines the program required and formed the cross-functional team responsible for execution.

My role

BI Project Manager

I was accountable for shaping the delivery organization and taking the program through execution, integration and production launch.

  • Built and led the multidisciplinary project team.
  • Coordinated engineering, BI, analysis and data-science workstreams.
  • Managed delivery expectations with ministry stakeholders and the executive sponsor.
  • Led the complete platform into production.
Program complexity

National scope, specialist users and multiple delivery layers.

The program combined public-sector stakeholder alignment with nationwide organizational coverage and a full BI delivery stack.

Success depended on coordinating people, requirements, data integration and analytical delivery as one program rather than separate technical projects.

Platform scope

From data integration to decision-ready information.

The team delivered the complete analytical chain—from source integration and warehousing to semantic models and user-facing BI products.
