Global Sourcing Analytics Modernization

I led the end-to-end delivery of a new global sourcing data warehouse and Power BI product, built from day zero to support an enterprise S/4HANA migration.

The mandate

Build the new sourcing analytics foundation without interrupting the existing service.

The ERP migration created a fixed business need: a new analytics product had to be designed for the future S/4HANA landscape while the previous dashboard continued to serve operational users.

The solution needed to support sourcing decisions across delivery performance, cost and payables, quality, and rejections—with stakeholders ranging from factory users to vice presidents.

Why it was complex

One product, seven divisions, different operational realities.

Global alignment

Seven divisions and 25 companies needed shared sourcing definitions without losing necessary local context.

Data and ERP change

SAP data, S/4HANA migration, data quality and the new analytical model had to evolve together.

Distributed delivery

Specialists across China, India, Bulgaria, Czechia and Belgium worked through cross-team dependencies.

Service continuity

The existing dashboard needed to remain operational throughout design, build and production launch.

My role

Project Lead, from day zero to production

I was accountable for delivery end to end and also performed the business-analysis leadership needed to shape the product.

  • Defined scope, roadmap, backlog, budget and the delivery plan.
  • Led a six-person cross-functional team spanning data engineering, BI development, data modelling, S/4HANA, SAP and business-process expertise.
  • Translated sourcing needs into product requirements and coordinated application, data and business dependencies.
  • Resolved KPI-definition decisions jointly with vice presidents when divisional expectations differed.
Solution architecture

A governed path from ERP source to sourcing decisions.

The delivery combined a sourcing-focused Databricks warehouse with a Power BI presentation layer designed around the agreed global KPIs.

Hybrid governance with iterative delivery.

I combined milestone-based governance for ERP and executive dependencies with an iterative backlog for analysis, engineering, BI development, validation and stakeholder feedback.
