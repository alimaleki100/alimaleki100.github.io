ICADB: National Agricultural Master Data Platform

Directing the technical architecture and phased implementation of the Iran Comprehensive Agriculture Database (ICADB), consolidating 31 provincial silos into a unified master data management lake.

Role
Technical PM & Data Architect
Entity
Ministry of Agriculture & ICT
Timeframe
2018 – 2020
Scope
31 Provincial Departments
01 / Context & National Scale Challenge

Fragmented Provincial Silos & Inconsistent Agricultural Baselines

National agricultural planning, subsidy distribution, and food reserve forecasting were historically hampered by extreme data fragmentation. Over 31 provincial agricultural directorates maintained autonomous, non-interoperable databases with conflicting taxonomies, unstandardized producer identifiers, and duplicate land registries.

Federal policymakers faced month-long reconciliation lags when attempting to estimate crop yields, allocate water rights, or balance strategic grain storage across regions.

02 / Technical Architecture & Governance

Unified Master Data Lake & Cross-Agency Semantic Standards

As Technical Project Manager and Data Architect, I spearheaded the technical roadmap, data model design, and multi-agency deployment:

  • Semantic Master Data Management (MDM): Established a standardized national ontology for crop types, producer identities, cadastral parcel mapping, and yield metrics.
  • Automated Ingestion & De-duplication: Built robust ETL ingestion pipelines equipped with probabilistic record matching and entity resolution algorithms to purge duplicate producer records.
  • Enterprise Data Lake Architecture: Implemented centralized repository housing multi-terabyte historical and transactional datasets accessible via role-governed APIs.
  • Policy Decision Support Dashboards: Deployed executive analytics dashboards providing federal planners with real-time visibility into regional commodity supply balances.
