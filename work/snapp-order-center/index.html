Snapp · Data Product

Snapp Order Center Data Backbone

I led the data product workstream behind a SuperApp feature that brought orders from multiple Snapp ventures into one customer experience.

Data Product Manager5-engineer teamHigh-volume dataCross-venture delivery
Challenge

One place for orders across the SuperApp.

Customers could place orders through different ventures such as Cab, Box, Shop, Market and Doctor, but the SuperApp needed a unified Order Center experience backed by dependable cross-venture data.

Scope

I owned the data side—not the application build.

My accountability covered the data product and the team delivering it. The application feature was owned by a separate development organization, which made cross-team alignment a core part of the work.

Delivery

A high-volume path from venture systems to the application.

The product used event pipelines, high-volume storage and application-facing data services, with observability around the delivery flow. I led the five-engineer data team, shaped the backlog and coordinated with application product managers and venture counterparts.

Dependency

Not every venture was ready to provide data.

Source readiness became a product dependency, not only an engineering issue. I exposed blockers, sequenced integrations and kept the central workstream moving while autonomous ventures progressed at different speeds.

Outcome

A production data product at SuperApp scale.

  • ClickHouse storage and pipelines built from scratch for cross-venture order data.
  • APIs and data services made order information available to the application team.
  • Quality monitoring and observability supported a data estate of hundreds of millions of records.
